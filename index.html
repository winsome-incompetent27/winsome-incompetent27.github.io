🛠️ How Does It Work?

Here's a simple breakdown of the magic behind cleetus:

  1. You point it at your project. Fire up cleetus in your project folder.
  2. It scans and understands. cleetus reads your source code, configuration files, and comments to build a context map.
  3. You ask for help. Type a request like "Write a function to parse CSV files" or "Explain this error".
  4. Local AI responds. cleetus sends your request to your chosen local backend (llama.cpp, etc.), which generates a response entirely on your machine. The answer appears right in your terminal, formatted with syntax highlighting.

The backend models are small enough to run on a normal laptop. You don't need a supercomputer – just a modern processor and 8GB+ of RAM is fine for smaller models.
